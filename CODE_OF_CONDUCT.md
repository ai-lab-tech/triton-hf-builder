# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in triton-hf-builder discussions, issues, pull requests
and reviews is expected to follow these rules.

## Participation

- Discuss technical choices with respect for the people involved.
- Welcome questions and contributions from people with different backgrounds
  and levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and accept that maintainers may decline a proposal.
- Avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats and sexual content
  directed at other participants.
- Respect privacy. Never publish another person's private messages, contact
  details or other personal information without their permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and do not repeatedly disrupt other contributors.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through a contact
method on their GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive details in a public
issue. Report software vulnerabilities using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers review reports, hear the people involved where appropriate, and
choose a proportionate response. Responses can include removing content,
requesting a change in behavior, or restricting participation. A maintainer
involved in a report should leave its assessment to another maintainer.

Reports should be handled discreetly. Retaliation against someone for reporting
a concern is unacceptable.
